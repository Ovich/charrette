# Editorial template: cover letter

The body of a cover letter for one job posting, written from the candidate's evidence, its
wording drawn from their earlier letters, read by the hiring manager it is for. Seven seats.

## When

A candidate applies to a posting and hands over: the posting; a structured profile of their
works, roles and facts with their constraints; the CV made for this application; their earlier
letters for roles of the same kind. One dossier per application, kept across its runs.

## The strategy

The protocol's defaults hold for the rest.

| field | value | why |
|---|---|---|
| questions | the body of a cover letter to <company> for <role>, in the posting's language | |
| evidence | `given`: the profile, the CV, the posting, the precedents; `web` for the judge's research only | |
| election | slots `hook`, `core` (the turn to the posting's core theme), `close`; source `precedents`, the facts from the profile and the CV | the candidate already approved those wordings |
| altitude | strategic | |
| criteria | the reader shortlists; every claim held by the evidence; the candidate's preferences kept; sounds written for this company | |

## The documents

The dossier adds, inside **Inputs**:

| input | what it is | binding |
|---|---|---|
| the profile | the candidate's works, roles and facts, with their constraints | every fact and constraint binds |
| the CV for this application | a source of proofs | the letter says what the CV leaves out, never restates it |
| the posting | the reader's own document | the reader's own words |
| the precedents | the candidate's earlier letters, closest first | wording and framing only |

## The seats

| seat | function | persona | effort |
|---|---|---|---|
| judge | judge | the company's recruiter judging the posting against the profile, from the record alone | low |
| coach | strategist | the career coach, on the candidate's side; the candidate's own sentence for what the reader keeps binds; the brief names what is left to the CV and the job interview | medium |
| writer | writer | the hiring manager for this role, writing for the candidate they want to hire | medium |
| recruiter | auditor | the senior recruiter, on the candidate's side; selling and natural language are priority 1 | medium |
| checker | checker | the profile's facts and `constraints`, the CV, the confirmed facts; a claim a constraint forbids is blocking | low |
| screener | reader | the company's hiring manager for this team, tired, hundreds of applications this month, under a minute per letter; knows only the posting and the letter; verdict `shortlist: yes / no / borderline` | low |
| style | style | the posting's voice: its register, warmth, own vocabulary and named values | low |

## The interview

Before the first run, into the dossier:

1. **The judge's table first**, each gap one open question about the work done.
2. **The motivation**: why this company, now.
3. **The one thing the reader keeps**, in the candidate's own sentence.
4. **The preferences**: voice (first person or not), register and warmth, the nod to the
   company's culture, level of detail, the path's starting point, the close. Proposed, then
   confirmed:
   - open on the strongest claim, the company's moment in a clause;
   - one closing sentence of value for the company, ending on a strength, no invitation;
   - a personal project without users told by its technical work, not as a product;
   - one warm nod, no pun.
5. **The precedents**: their earlier letters for roles of the same kind, at most ten.
6. **The company research**, into the dossier's given research, each fact with its source and
   date.

## The output

The posting's language, the body only, one page, four paragraphs, 250–280 words; the frame
(salutation, closing formula, attachments) is the candidate's.

| slot | must do | limit | elected |
|---|---|---|---|
| hook | the strongest claim, the company's moment in a clause, the one warm nod | 2 sentences, ~50 words | yes |
| path | where the path starts, the works that prove it, the principle they show | ~95 words | no |
| core | the posting's core theme: the candidate's work on it, its traction | ~65 words | yes |
| support and close | the work that supports the core, by its engineering; one sentence of value for the company | ~65 words, the close ≤ 25 | close |

## What the orchestrator watches

- **A work introduced twice**: each work has one place, the most it does there.
- **Jargon the first reader cannot place**, and a date said twice.
- **A colon formula** (« Ce que X gagne : … »).
- **An example letter written for this posting given as input**: the seats write from the
  profile, the CV and the precedents only.
