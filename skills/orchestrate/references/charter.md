# The charter

Ask what is open in one message, the recommended answer first. Write the answers into the
kind's document, in its charter section, as `field: value` lines, and into its one line, where
the kind keeps one. **Only the person amends the charter**: a field you think should change is a pause, with your reason, never a decision of yours. An amendment mid-work overwrites its field in both places, the decision's id
beside it (`pace: run through, the person merges (D41)`), and reaches the next brief only.

The fields, their order and their recommendations are the kind's skill's.
