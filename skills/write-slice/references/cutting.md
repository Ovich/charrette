# Cutting an increment into slices

- **Thin and vertical**, through every layer the change touches, each verifiable end to end when it lands. By layer only when one check holds every step, an interface that must not change held by the suite, and the plan says so.
- **Per slice**: the stories it serves (`US3`), what it delivers end to end, the slices that block it, its check as a command the orchestrator can run, the tests it owes, and a mark when it needs a person: `👤 decision`, `👤 design review`, or none.
- **When a slice's tests are written is the mandate's `tests`.** A slice whose nature wants another rhythm names it in its heading, `tests: <rhythm>`, in the words of the `execute-plan` skill's `references/orchestrator-mandate.md`: a rework held by the suite as it stands is *at the slice end* whatever the mandate says.
- **Under `tests: at the plan end` the cut ends on a test slice**, the last before the plan's `👤` checks, blocked by every other slice. Each slice still names its seams and its cases: they are what the test slice writes from. Each slice also names, among its acceptance criteria, the proof that stands in for its tests until then: a story of the verify skill, a walk of the screen or a command whose output shows the behaviour. The test slice's document lists the slice documents it collects from, and nothing of the code.
- **One slice is one agent's context window.** Riskiest unknown first.
- **Slices that block nothing of each other's may fork**, on the terms the `execute-plan` skill's `references/tracker.md` sets. Propose the fork in one message, draw it on a yes.
- **A slice that lands on `main` leaves it green**: complete, or inert where it is not yet, and the plan names what makes it inert.
- **A decision a slice needs that cannot be made before an earlier slice runs** cuts that slice to "planned after slice N runs".
