# Cutting an increment into slices

- **Thin and vertical**, through every layer the change touches, each verifiable end to end when it lands. By layer only when one check holds every step, an interface that must not change held by the suite, and the plan says so.
- **One slice per seat per phase**; slices of one phase never block each other and may share a file, settled on the board.
- **Per slice**: the stories it serves (`US3`), what it delivers end to end, its check as a command the orchestrator can run, and a mark when it needs a person: `👤 decision`, `👤 design review`, or none.
- **Under `tests: at the plan end` the cut ends on a tests phase**, one slice per seat, the last before the plan's `👤` checks.
- **One slice is one agent's context window.** Riskiest unknown first.
- **A slice that lands on `main` leaves it green**: complete, or inert where it is not yet, and the plan names what makes it inert.
- **A decision a slice needs that cannot be made before an earlier slice runs** puts that slice in a later phase.

| Thought | Reality |
|---|---|
| "Schema first, then the API, then the screens" | Nothing is verifiable until the last slice. |
